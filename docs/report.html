1. What the classifier does

For every student answer, the website compares the recognised text with each key point of the teacher's model answer and gives one of three verdicts:

VerdictMeaningMarks
SupportsThe answer states what the key point states.Full marks for that key point
ContradictsThe answer states the opposite (for example, a swapped gas or direction).Zero
NeitherOff topic, incomplete, or too vague to decide.Zero, and flagged for the teacher

3. Model and training

Base modelcross-encoder/nli-distilroberta-base: a 6-layer DistilRoBERTa (about 82 million parameters) already trained on general English entailment (entailment / contradiction / neutral). Those three outputs were reused as supports / contradicts / neither.
InputThe pair (student answer, model answer key point), up to 128 tokens
Training settings2 epochs, learning rate 2×10-5, batch size 32, 564 steps

How to read this. The model is right about 7 times in 10 on questions it has never seen. It is weakest on "contradicts": it finds about 4 to 5 of every 10 contradicting answers. This is why the website flags every uncertain or "neither" verdict for the teacher, and why the teacher can change any verdict. The biology check has only 12 pairs, so its 75% is a rough indication, not a precise score.

Check on the website: demo biology quiz

Three simulated students' handwritten sheets (5 questions, 11 marks) were scanned in simulation, read by the handwriting reader and marked, first with the untrained model and then with the trained one.

Training fixed the correct and partly correct answers. Both models still miss answers that use the right words in the wrong roles ("plants take in oxygen and release carbon dioxide"), so the teacher's review is still needed for those. Adding such swapped-term pairs to the biology training data is the most direct fix.

Ways to raise the accuracy

  • Train on a GPU (Colab or Kaggle) for 3 to 4 epochs instead of 2.
  • Use a larger base model such as cross-encoder/nli-deberta-v3-base (GPU recommended).
  • Add more biology pairs from your own syllabus, especially contradicting answers.
  • Add the verdicts teachers corrected on the website (data/corrections.csv) to the training data.

Example battery. A 12 V, 2,000 mAh pack holds 24 Wh. Using about 80% of it (19 Wh) gives roughly 850 sheets, or about 3.5 hours of continuous scanning, per charge. The battery must deliver at least 0.5 A continuously; 1 A or more is advised, because current rises briefly whenever a move starts.

Assumptions to check. The battery voltage, the motor's coil resistance and the DRV8825 current limit (set with the potentiometer, Vref = current limit ÷ 2) are not recorded in the code. Motor power scales with the square of the current limit: at 0.7 A per phase, the motors use about half the power shown here. To lower consumption further, the firmware could energise only the motor that is needed for each move, and release the motors sooner while the sheet is held.
